Presage Party

Social party games where your body is the controller.

What this app reads

  • Breathing amplitude (from your face)
  • Blink and talking detection
  • Breathing rate
Privacy by design
  • Camera and video never leave your device
  • Only derived metrics are used for gameplay
  • No accounts, no server-side biometric storage
  • SDK telemetry is disabled

This build uses replay mode: a pre-recorded session is played back through the same pipeline. No live camera is accessed.
