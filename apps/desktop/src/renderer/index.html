Keep your composure. Last calm player wins.

Privacy by design
  • Camera and video never leave your device
  • Only derived metrics are used for gameplay
  • No accounts, no server-side biometric storage
  • SDK telemetry is disabled

This build uses replay mode. No live camera is accessed.

How to Play

Welcome to Flinch Royale. Your goal is simple: maintain your composure for 30 seconds.

Core Mechanics

  • Composure: Starts at 100%. If it reaches 0%, you are eliminated.
  • Flinching (Blinks): Every blink reduces your composure. Keep your eyes steady.
  • Talking: Sustained vocalization will drain your composure rapidly.
  • Heart Rate: If your biometric reading spikes above the threshold, you will lose composure over time.

Scoring

  • Final Score = Composure × 10. Survive 30 seconds with 100% composure to achieve the maximum 1000 points.
  • If your composure reaches 0% before the timer ends, your score is 0 — you did not survive.
  • Scores are comparable across modes. Higher difficulty modes naturally produce lower composure and therefore lower scores — a top score in Don't Scream is worth more than the same score in Chill Mode.

Game Modes

  • Chill Mode: The baseline test. No external distractions.
  • Seriously?: Adds unpredictable computer-generated auditory and visual distractors designed to break your focus.
  • Why So Serious?: The ultimate test. Replaces standard distractors with a randomized video challenge. You must endure without laughing or flinching. No skip controls are provided.
  • Don't Scream: A horror experience. Prepare for sudden, intense audiovisual jumpscares. Blinking or a heart rate spike immediately after a scare will result in severe composure loss.
