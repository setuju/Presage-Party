🕹️ The Composure Arcade Challenge ABOUT
Origin & Mission:

Flinch Royale was designed and developed for the Presage SmartSpectra SDK Challenge. Combining the nostalgia of classic 8-bit retro arcade aesthetics with cutting-edge optical and biometric telemetry, it turns facial composure into a competitive multiplayer sport.

💡 Technology Architecture
🛡️ SmartSpectra SDK Principles Derived metrics (blink frequency, speech cues, breathing stability) are computed locally without storing or streaming raw video feeds to external servers.
🔒 Zero-Cloud Video Privacy Webcam frames are processed directly inside your browser memory using high-performance optical flow differential analysis.
⚡ Multiplayer Synchronization Low-latency Socket.IO orchestrates room matching, guest challenges, anti-cheat detection, and synchronized distraction reel playlists.
🎨 8-Bit SatuRumah Theme Pixel borders, hard-edge shadows, and the warm "Sunset Over a Pixel Village" color palette provide seamless visual unity across all devices.
