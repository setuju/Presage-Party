🎮 Complete Player Survival Guide ARCADE RULES
Objective:

Flinch Royale is a real-time biometric and optical composure game. Maintain unbroken focus, do not flinch, do not talk, and do not blink excessively while withstanding psychological distractors.

⚡ The Composure Meter (100% / 1000 Pts)

Every round starts with 100% Composure (equal to 1,000 points on the global scoreboard). Involuntary facial reflexes drain your composure:

💥 Flinch / Head Motion (-6%) Sudden jerking, nodding, or looking away is caught by frame-by-frame optical flow analysis.
👁️ Eye Blinks (-4%) Excessive rapid blink bursts deduct composure. Keep your eyes steady on the target!
🗣️ Talking / Laughing (-8%) Gasping, laughing, or talking triggers acoustic/motion detection.
🚫 Focus / Tab Loss (-10%) Leaving or minimizing the browser tab triggers the anti-cheat composure penalty.
🎯 The 4 Game Modes
🟢 Level 1: Chill Mode (Baseline) Pure composure endurance without active audio or video distractors. Great for calibrating your camera.
🟡 Level 2: Seriously? (Audio Cues) Sudden speech synthesis, car horns, phone rings, and spoken questions ("Look behind you!", "Are you nervous?").
🔴 Level 3: Why So Serious? (Viral Reels) Synchronized vertical video stream that automatically advances to the next reel once a clip finishes.
🔥 Level 4: Don't Scream (Horror Spikes) Sudden jumpscare spikes trigger at unpredictable intervals with -15% composure penalties for post-scare flinches.
👥 Multiplayer Room Master Rules

• The 👑 ROOM MASTER chooses the exact Game Mode (1 to 4) and Round Duration (30s, 60s, or 180s).

• When Mode 3 is picked, the random playlist seed is distributed across all players so everyone watches and reacts to the identical reel at the exact same instant.

• Match scores are submitted automatically to the 5-Division Leaderboard upon round completion.
