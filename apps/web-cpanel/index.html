Game Helper — How to Play Presage Party
  • Levels 1–4 & Tempo: Higher levels increase orb movement speed, distractions, and scoring multiplier.
  • Focus Math & Reflexes: Tap the green TAP FOCUS orb while solving quick math prompts in the right panel to earn bonus points and recover composure.
  • Composure, Blink, Talk & Rate: Keep Composure above 0. Flinching, frequent blinks (-4), sustained talking (-8), unstable rate, or switching tabs/minimizing penalizes Composure.
  • Room Master Rule: Only the 👑 ROOM MASTER (creator) can trigger Start Game for the room. Joined players (🎮 GUEST) wait for the Room Master or play Solo when not in a room.
  • Camera Transparency: Camera preview runs locally in your browser (no frames uploaded to server, no biometric data stored). Simulation triggers (flinch/blink/talk) still occur even when camera is off.
