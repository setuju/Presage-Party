🛡️ Privacy Policy & Data Transparency LEGAL
Privacy by Design Promise:

Your privacy is paramount. Flinch Royale was designed around zero-cloud telemetry principles. No webcam video is ever streamed, stored, or analyzed on remote servers.

📸 Camera Processing Stays 100% Local
No Video Transmission Webcam frames are analyzed entirely within your browser's private sandbox memory. No video streams or raw frames are ever transmitted over the network.
Local Biometrics Blinks, mouth movement, and head flinches are computed strictly on-device using HTML5 Canvas pixel differential algorithms.
Revocable Anytime You can toggle off camera access at any moment using the in-game camera button or your browser permissions bar.
No Medical Claims Flinch Royale is an entertainment game. No medical diagnoses, health profiling, or biometric identification is performed.
🌐 Multiplayer & Leaderboard Data

The only information coordinated with our server and recorded on the leaderboard is:

• Your chosen player nickname.

• Your numerical composure score (0 to 1,000 points).

• Your multiplayer room code and host/guest status.

• A timestamp of when the score was achieved.

🍪 Local Storage & Cache API

We use native browser storage solely to:

• Remember your preferred 8-bit color theme (light / dark).

• Cache the Level 3 distraction videos locally so you do not have to re-download them each session.

• Keep your personal scoreboard history accessible offline.
